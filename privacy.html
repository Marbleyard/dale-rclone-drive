Privacy Notice

Dale rclone Drive

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Dale Yanda operates Dale rclone Drive for his own Google Drive file and project workflows.

Information accessed and purpose

The connection accesses file and folder metadata, such as names, identifiers, and locations, and file contents needed for the owner’s requested tasks. It uses Google authorization tokens to make permitted Drive requests. Account information may be used to confirm the connected account.

The connection uses this information to find and read files, download selected files for processing, upload results, and carry out other file changes requested by the owner. The Google Drive permission permits broader file management, including moving and deleting accessible files.

AI tools and data handling

Claude Code (Anthropic) and Google Antigravity (Google) may receive file contents, metadata, instructions, and results needed to perform the owner’s tasks. Other coordinating tools may receive task information when the owner directs their use. Those services process information according to their applicable terms, privacy policies, and account settings. Processing can take place both on the Mac and through these services.

The app does not sell Google Drive data or use it for advertising.

Provider information: Anthropic Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy, and rclone Privacy Policy. Service-specific terms and account agreements also apply.

Storage and retention

Google Drive stores the original files and uploaded results according to the account’s file and sharing settings. Authorization credentials are stored in the local rclone configuration on the Mac. Task-specific downloads, working files, and logs may also be stored locally until removed. AI services may retain task information according to their policies and account settings.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke this app’s Google access through the Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revocation prevents future authorized requests through that grant; it does not automatically erase downloaded files, logs, or information already retained by an AI service.

The owner can delete Drive files, local working copies, and local credentials as appropriate, and use each AI service’s controls for information retained there.
