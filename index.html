Personal Google Drive connection

Dale rclone Drive

Dale rclone Drive is Dale Yanda’s personal Google Drive connection. It uses rclone on his Mac to carry out file and project tasks through his authorized Google account, daleyanda@gmail.com.

What the connection does

Claude Code and Google Antigravity use this connection for owner-directed tasks, including finding files, reading documents, downloading selected source files, and uploading results to Google Drive.

The app requests Google Drive access through Google’s consent screen. File access follows the permissions of the signed-in account, including files shared with that account. The authorized Drive permission can also support changes, moves, and deletions of accessible files when directed by the owner.

Data handling

Files and related information selected for a task may be processed by the AI tools used for that task. The Privacy Notice describes access, processing, storage, and revocation.

This connection is intended for Dale’s use and is not offered as a public file-management service. This information website itself does not provide a Drive login or access to Drive files.
